# Colorado Ballot Explorer: Product Requirements

Source: docs/vision.md · Status: draft for review · Generated by /anvil-prd

## Problem

Colorado voters get their mail ballots weeks before Election Day, but understanding the whole ballot is hard. Per the vision, a single Boulder ballot in 2026 carries federal and state races, 14 statewide measures, 13 judicial retentions, county and city races, and special district questions. The information exists across the Blue Book, county sample ballots, city packets and candidate sites, but nobody puts it together completely. Existing tools miss most local items: a Boulder worksheet built from news coverage missed six minor-party candidates, seven appellate judges and every local measure outside the City of Boulder. As a result, many voters decide down-ballot items in the booth or skip them.

## Goals

1. A Colorado voter can see every contest and question that could be on their ballot from a ZIP code, and the exact set for their location, in under five minutes on a phone.
2. Every item shown is either verified against the county's official sample ballot or clearly labeled as not verified.
3. The voter can record decisions and notes, and produce a short write-out in printed ballot order to use while voting.
4. No choice, note, address or location ever leaves the voter's device unless the voter exports it.
5. Organizations across the political spectrum are willing to link to it because its presentation is demonstrably neutral.

## Non-goals

Carried from the vision's "What we won't do":
- Accounts, logins, or collecting personal information.
- Storing choices or notes on a server.
- Selling, sharing or analyzing data.
- Recommending candidates or positions, ranking options, or "match" quizzes.
- Showing a partial ballot as if it were complete.
- Ads or third-party tracking.

Additional scope cuts for the pilot and Release 1:
- States other than Colorado.
- Primaries and special elections (open question Q3).
- Measure descriptions written by the project. Measures show official text and link to official analysis until a cross-spectrum review panel exists (open question Q5).
- In-app summaries of opinions or endorsements.
- Voter registration, ballot tracking, or anything that requires identifying the voter.

## Users

- **Thorough voter:** wants to understand the whole ballot, researches over days, talks with family. Needs notes and a place to keep their thinking.
- **New or recently moved voter:** doesn't know their districts. Needs ZIP lookup with honest location caveats, and help narrowing.
- **Kitchen-table mail voter:** fills in the paper ballot at home. Needs the write-out in the same order as the printed ballot.
- **Vote center voter:** votes in person, often on a phone with poor signal. Needs offline access and a cheat sheet they made themselves.
- **Shared-device voter:** uses a library or family computer. Needs a mode that keeps nothing.

## Functional requirements

Ballot lookup
- REQ-01: Lookup by Colorado ZIP code (research mode) shows every contest and question that could appear on ballots in that ZIP, marking items that depend on exact location with the district they depend on.
- REQ-02: A non-Colorado ZIP shows a short message that the app covers Colorado only, with a link to that state's official election site.
- REQ-03: Precise mode narrows to the ballot for one location, using either the device's location or a point the voter taps on a map, with the district check done on the device.
- REQ-04: Guided narrowing lets voters who know their city, county or districts trim the list with pickers, without entering an address or location.
- REQ-05: Items appear in official ballot order, grouped as the printed ballot groups them: federal, state, legislature, judges, county, city, special districts, then measures.

Research
- REQ-06: Each item links to research sources in a fixed order: official sources first (Blue Book, GoVoteColorado, county sample ballot and TABOR notice, full text), then nonpartisan explainers, then candidates' own sites and filings. For judicial retentions, the official source is the judge's page from the Colorado Office of Judicial Performance Evaluation, linked without quoting or displaying its finding.
- REQ-07: For measures, links to the organized support and opposition campaigns always appear together, side by side, when either exists.
- REQ-08: Each measure shows its official ballot text exactly as certified (Blue Book ballot title for statewide measures, the county's published ballot question for local measures) and links to the official analysis. The app shows no summary or description written by the project.

Decisions and notes
- REQ-09: Measures and judicial retentions have a three-position control: Yes, Undecided (default), No.
- REQ-10: Candidate contests allow selecting candidates up to the contest's vote limit and prevent selecting more; ranked-choice contests allow ranking instead.
- REQ-11: The voter can add a free-text note to any item.
- REQ-12: The ballot shows progress: how many items are decided out of the total shown.

Take it with you
- REQ-13: "Write out my ballot" produces a short list of decisions in printed ballot order, with options to include or exclude notes and undecided items, that can be copied, printed or saved.
- REQ-14: The app shows Colorado voting basics: ballots must be received (not postmarked) by 7 p.m. on Election Day, with links to official drop box and vote center locators.

Storage and portability
- REQ-15: Choices and notes are saved automatically in the browser and survive a reload.
- REQ-16: A "Clear everything" control removes all saved choices, notes and settings after confirmation.
- REQ-17: Private session mode keeps nothing after the tab closes.
- REQ-18: "Save to file" exports choices and notes to a file the voter controls; "Open file" imports it on another device.
- REQ-19: Choices can move to another device by a QR code generated and read on the devices, with no server involved.

Trust
- REQ-20: Each county shows a coverage label: "Verified complete" with source and date, or "Statewide items only".
- REQ-21: Each item shows provenance: a link to its official source and the date it was verified.
- REQ-22: A visible "Report an error" path and a public changelog of data corrections.
- REQ-23: A plain-language privacy page describes exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

## Non-functional requirements

- NFR-01: Privacy. No ZIP, address, location, choice or note is ever sent over the network. The only network requests are to the site's own origin for app files and data files. Every voter's browser requests the same set of data files, so the host cannot infer county or district from which files were fetched.
- NFR-02: No third parties. No analytics, ad tech, third-party scripts, third-party fonts, cookies or fingerprinting. A strict Content Security Policy allows only the site's own origin, with integrity checks on every script.
- NFR-03: Offline. After the first visit, the app and the ballot data for every county work with no connection.
- NFR-04: Performance. On a mid-range phone over a 4G connection, the ballot for a ZIP is visible within 3 seconds of submitting it on first visit and within 1 second on repeat visits. (Targets proposed, not from the vision.)
- NFR-05: Accessibility. Meets WCAG 2.2 AA, including full keyboard and screen-reader operation of every control.
- NFR-06: Neutral presentation. Candidates appear in official ballot order with identical fields and treatment for every candidate, including minor parties and write-in lines. No reordering by party or popularity.
- NFR-07: Data integrity. Every data file is versioned, validates against the published schema, and has a published hash; every item carries source and verified date.
- NFR-08: Verifiability. Code and data are open source with a reproducible build, so anyone can confirm the deployed site matches the source.
- NFR-09: Simplicity. Usable at 360 px screen width with no setup; a first-time voter can reach their ballot in under one minute.

## Acceptance criteria

Lookup
- AC-01: WHEN a voter enters a valid Colorado ZIP, the system SHALL show all contests and questions for every ballot style that intersects that ZIP. (REQ-01)
- AC-02: WHEN an item applies to only part of the ZIP, the system SHALL label it with the district it depends on, for example "If you're in State House District 10". (REQ-01)
- AC-03: IF a voter enters a valid ZIP outside Colorado, THEN the system SHALL show the Colorado-only message and a link to that state's official election site, and SHALL NOT show any ballot items. (REQ-02)
- AC-04: WHEN a voter grants device location or taps a point on the map, the system SHALL show only the items whose districts contain that point. (REQ-03)
- AC-05: WHILE precise mode is in use, the system SHALL make no network request that contains coordinates or an address. (REQ-03, NFR-01)
- AC-06: WHEN a voter selects a city, county or district in guided narrowing, the system SHALL remove items that cannot apply to that selection. (REQ-04)
- AC-07: WHEN the ballot is displayed, the system SHALL order groups and items exactly as the county's official sample ballot orders them. (REQ-05, NFR-06)

Research
- AC-08: WHEN a voter opens an item, the system SHALL show official source links before any other links. (REQ-06)
- AC-09: WHEN a measure has a support or opposition campaign link, the system SHALL show both sides in the same row, with "No organized campaign found" for a missing side. (REQ-07)
- AC-10: WHEN a voter opens a measure, the system SHALL show its certified ballot text and a link to the official analysis, and SHALL NOT show any summary written by the project. (REQ-08)
- AC-11: IF a measure's ballot text does not match the certified text recorded in its county or statewide verification record, THEN the build SHALL fail. (REQ-08, NFR-07)
- AC-41: WHEN a voter opens a judicial retention item, the system SHALL link to that judge's official performance evaluation and SHALL NOT display the evaluation's finding in the app. (REQ-06)

Decisions
- AC-12: WHEN a measure or retention item is first shown, the system SHALL set its control to Undecided. (REQ-09)
- AC-13: IF a voter tries to select more candidates than the vote limit, THEN the system SHALL keep the selection unchanged and say how many may be chosen. (REQ-10)
- AC-14: WHEN a contest is ranked-choice, the system SHALL let the voter assign unique ranks up to the number of candidates. (REQ-10)
- AC-15: WHEN a voter types a note, the system SHALL save it within 1 second without a save button. (REQ-11, REQ-15)
- AC-16: WHEN any decision changes, the system SHALL update the "X of Y decided" count. (REQ-12)

Take it with you
- AC-17: WHEN a voter opens the write-out, the system SHALL list decided items in printed ballot order with one line per item. (REQ-13)
- AC-18: WHEN a voter toggles "include notes" or "include undecided", the system SHALL update the write-out accordingly. (REQ-13)
- AC-19: WHEN a voter chooses print, the system SHALL produce a print layout of the write-out without navigation or other page chrome. (REQ-13)
- AC-20: WHEN the ballot or write-out is shown, the system SHALL show the "received by 7 p.m. on Election Day" reminder and official locator links. (REQ-14)

Storage
- AC-21: WHEN a voter reloads the page outside private session mode, the system SHALL restore all choices and notes. (REQ-15)
- AC-22: WHEN a voter confirms "Clear everything", the system SHALL delete all stored app data so that a reload shows no choices, notes or settings. (REQ-16)
- AC-23: WHILE private session mode is on, the system SHALL write nothing to persistent browser storage. (REQ-17)
- AC-24: WHEN a voter imports a file exported from the app, the system SHALL restore the same choices and notes. (REQ-18)
- AC-25: IF an imported file is malformed or from an unknown version, THEN the system SHALL reject it with a plain message and leave existing data unchanged. (REQ-18)
- AC-26: WHEN a voter scans a transfer QR code from another device, the system SHALL import the choices with no network request. (REQ-19, NFR-01)

Trust
- AC-27: WHEN a county's local items are not verified, the system SHALL show "Statewide items only" for that county and SHALL NOT show unverified local items as complete. (REQ-20)
- AC-28: WHEN an item is shown, the system SHALL show its source link and verified date. (REQ-21, NFR-07)
- AC-29: WHEN a voter chooses "Report an error", the system SHALL open a report path that does not require an account on the site. (REQ-22)
- AC-30: WHEN a data correction is published, the changelog SHALL list the item, the change and the date. (REQ-22)
- AC-31: WHEN a voter opens the privacy page, the system SHALL describe storage, export, network requests and hosting logs in plain language. (REQ-23)

Quality
- AC-32: WHEN any page loads, the system SHALL make network requests only to its own origin. (NFR-01, NFR-02)
- AC-33: WHEN any page loads, the system SHALL set no cookies and load no third-party resources. (NFR-02)
- AC-34: WHILE the device is offline after a prior visit, the system SHALL load the app and that county's ballot. (NFR-03)
- AC-35: WHEN measured on a mid-range phone profile over 4G, the system SHALL show the ballot within the NFR-04 targets. (NFR-04)
- AC-36: WHEN audited with automated accessibility tools and a manual screen-reader pass, the system SHALL have no WCAG 2.2 AA violations. (NFR-05)
- AC-37: WHEN a data file is built, the build SHALL fail if it does not validate against the schema or lacks a hash. (NFR-07)
- AC-38: WHEN the deployed site is rebuilt from the tagged source, the system SHALL produce files with matching published hashes. (NFR-08)
- AC-39: WHEN shown at 360 px width, the system SHALL display the full ballot flow with no horizontal scrolling. (NFR-09)
- AC-40: WHEN a voter looks up any Colorado ZIP, the system SHALL request the same set of data files regardless of which ZIP was entered. (NFR-01)

## Success metrics

| Metric | Target | How collected (no analytics) |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage | Pilot: statewide items for all 64 counties, Boulder and Larimer verified complete. Release 1: all 64 verified. | Count of county verification records |
| Accuracy | Zero unreported mismatches against sample ballots; every reported error fixed within 48 hours before an election | Error reports and changelog |
| Usefulness | At least 4 of 5 pilot participants finish their ballot and produce a write-out unaided | Moderated user testing, voluntary feedback form |
| Trust | At least one partner from each side of the spectrum, or a nonpartisan civic org, links to the site | Partner outreach log |
| Privacy | Zero off-origin requests in automated tests and in an independent review | Test suite and published review |

## Release scope

**Pilot (starts October 16, 2026, for the November 3, 2026 general election; invited testers only).** Ballots went out October 2 (per vision). Starting the pilot on October 16 gives testers about two and a half weeks to use it on their real ballots before Election Day. The pilot is a user-testing release for a small invited group, not a public launch, and is not promoted to partners. Includes lookup and narrowing (REQ-01, REQ-02, REQ-04, REQ-05), research links and official measure text (REQ-06, REQ-07, REQ-08), decisions and notes (REQ-09 to REQ-12), write-out (REQ-13, REQ-14), local storage and private session (REQ-15 to REQ-17), coverage and provenance labels (REQ-20, REQ-21) and the privacy page (REQ-23). NFRs: all except NFR-08; NFR-05 is checked with automated tools only, and NFR-04 targets are measured and reported but do not block. Testers report errors directly to the project during moderated sessions. Data: statewide items for all counties, Boulder and Larimer verified complete.

**Release 1 (November 2, 2027 coordinated election, public).** Adds save and open file (REQ-18), the public error report path and changelog (REQ-22), precise mode (REQ-03), QR transfer (REQ-19), the manual screen-reader pass for NFR-05, reproducible-build verification (NFR-08), and all 64 counties verified. Lessons from the pilot feed a PRD revision before Release 1 planning.

## Open questions

- Q1: Resolved (critique D1). The 2026 cycle is an invited-tester pilot starting October 16, 2026; Release 1 targets the November 2027 coordinated election. See Release scope.
- Q2: Resolved (critique D5). Until a county is verified, it shows statewide items only, per AC-27. Licensed data labeled unverified is not used, because the vision rules out showing a partial ballot as if it were complete.
- Q3: How do primaries and special elections fit? The data model allows multiple elections, and Release 1 covers the 2027 odd-year coordinated election.
- Q4: Can the Secretary of State or clerks share ballot data or precinct ballot styles directly?
- Q5: Who would sit on a cross-spectrum review panel for project-written measure descriptions, and what is the process? Until a panel exists, REQ-08 limits measures to official text (critique D3). No panel members are identified yet.
- Q6: Resolved (critique D6). "Report an error" offers both email and a public GitHub issue, and the privacy page explains what each exposes. Applies from Release 1.
- Q7: Who hosts and funds it, and does the host's logging meet NFR-01?
- Q8: Lessons from Searchboard are still unwritten in the vision.
